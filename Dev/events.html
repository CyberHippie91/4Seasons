MURDER MYSTERY

MURDER MYSERTY SUPPER

7PM, 14TH NOVEMBER


Prepare yourself for an unforgettable evening of suspense, laughter, and intrigue! Our Murder Mystery Supper invites you to step into a world where nothing is quite as it seems. Imagine the charm of a classic Agatha Christie whodunnit infused with the cheeky comedy of Carry-On—a perfect mix of suspense and hilarity.

Join your fellow diners as you unravel a fiendishly clever plot. Work together with your tablemates to follow the clues, interrogate the suspects, and piece together the story behind the crime. Every detail counts, and every question could bring you closer to the truth… or send you down a deliciously misleading rabbit hole!

The stakes are high: the first table to crack the case wins a bottle of bubbly, so bring your sharpest wits and your best sleuthing skills. Along the way, enjoy a sumptuous supper, sip on your favorite drinks, and immerse yourself in a night of thrilling entertainment.

Will you be the detective who solves the mystery, or will the culprit slip away unnoticed? There’s only one way to find out—join us for an evening of murder, mirth, and magnificent food!
