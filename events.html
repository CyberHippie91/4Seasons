A FAULTY TOWERS DINNER

A FAULTY TOWERS DINNER

7PM, 25TH APRIL


Prepare for an evening of chaos, comedy, and classic British dining as Four Seasons Brasserie in Eastbourne Harbour presents a special “Faulty Towers Dinner” experience. Inspired by the iconic sitcom Fawlty Towers, this immersive event brings the unforgettable characters and hilarious mishaps to life around you as you dine.

From the moment you arrive, you’ll be part of the action, with plenty of surprises, quick-witted humour, and unpredictable moments throughout the evening. Taking place on Friday 25th April from 7pm, guests will enjoy a carefully prepared two-course meal for £30 per person, served alongside the live performance. Whether you’re a long-time fan of the show or simply looking for a unique night out, this promises to be an entertaining experience full of laughter.

A £10 deposit is required to secure your booking, and due to the nature of the event, spaces are limited and expected to fill quickly. Join us for a night where the service may be questionable, but the entertainment is guaranteed. To book your table, call 01323 470704, email fourseasonsbrasserie@outlook.com, or visit us at 4 Seasons Brasserie.
